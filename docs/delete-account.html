Delete your Aether Tides account

Aether Tides accounts are anonymous: there is no email or password, only a random player ID created the first time you played online.

From inside the game (fastest)

Open Settings → Delete Account, confirm, and it is done. This removes your player ID, display name, rating, and match history from our servers immediately. It cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the game installed

Email polarizenterprises@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account". Include your player ID if you know it (it was shown in Settings), or your display name and the approximate date you last played so we can find the account. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player account at our authentication provider
  • Your profile: display name, rating, matches played, matches won, best distance
  • Your match history

What is not deleted

  • Server logs containing the IP address your device used, which are kept for up to 30 days for

    • abuse prevention and are not linked to your account

  • Aggregate, non-identifying analytics that were never tied to your account

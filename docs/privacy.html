Aether Tides — Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 September 2026 · Version: 1
Operator: Polariz Enterprises ("we", "us") · Contact: polarizenterprises@gmail.com

This policy explains what Aether Tides (the "Game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read. If anything here is unclear, email us.

The short version

  • Playing offline (Practice mode) sends nothing anywhere.
  • Playing online creates an anonymous account: a random ID, no email, no password, no name

    • unless you type one.

  • We store your display name, rating, and match results so the game can pair you with

    • opponents of similar skill and show you your record.

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or anything from other apps.
  • You can delete your account and everything tied to it from inside the Game, at any time.

Crash and usage events never include your player ID, your display name, your opponent, or anything you typed.

During an online match, your control inputs (helm position, spell casts, hazard placements) are sent to our match server so the game can run. They are processed in memory and are not stored.

What we do not collect

Location. Contacts. Photos, media or files. Microphone or camera. Advertising identifiers. Email address or phone number. Payment information (the Game has no purchases). We do not use advertising SDKs and do not sell data to anyone.

Who we share it with

We use two service providers, who process data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Supabase, Inc. — authentication and database hosting.
  • Google LLC (Firebase) — crash reporting and usage analytics, if enabled in your build.

The match server is operated by us. We do not share personal data with anyone else, except if required by law.

Your display name and rating are visible to the opponent you are matched with, and may appear on a public leaderboard if you have played at least three online matches. Choose a name you are happy to have seen.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile (ID, name, rating, record): until you delete your account.
  • Match results: 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Server logs including IP addresses: up to 30 days.
  • Crash and usage data at Firebase: per Google's retention settings, at most 14 months.

Your choices and rights

Delete your account. Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, match history and display name from our systems immediately and cannot be undone. Practice mode keeps working. If you cannot access the Game, email us from any address and we will delete the account matching the player ID you give us.

Change your name. Settings → Name. The old name is not retained.

Play without an account. Practice mode never creates one.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA, India under the DPDP Act) you may also have rights to access, correct, port, or object to processing of your data, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. We will never ask you for a password, because you do not have one.

Children

Aether Tides is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to the match server and to the authentication service are encrypted (TLS). Account tokens are stored only in the Game's private storage on your device and are excluded from device backups. The match server verifies every action a client sends and holds the only copy of ratings and results; the Game cannot edit them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the Game will ask you to accept the new version before you play online again. The version number at the top will change.
